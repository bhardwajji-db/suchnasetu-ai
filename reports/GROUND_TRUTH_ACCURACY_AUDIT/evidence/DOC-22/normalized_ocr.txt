MINUTES OF THE 45TH GOVERNING COUNCIL MEETING

Held at Conference Hall B, New Delhi

The meeting was chaired by the Vice Chairman. Apologies for absence were received from Dr. Gupta.
The minutes of the previous meeting were reviewed and signed without amendment.

ITEM 4: PUBLIC ANNOUNCEMENT OF POSTDOCTORAL GRANT 2026
The Council resolved to approve the Postdoctoral Research Grant 2026.
Eligibility: Doctorate in Bioengineering obtained within past 3 years.
Application Fee: Nil (No fee required).

Final Application Closing Date: 10 June 2026.

Submissions to be made at: https://council.gov.in/grants

ITEM 5: PROCUREMENT OF LABORATORY EQUIPMENT
The tender for spectrophotometers was referred to the finance sub-committee for further audit.
Meeting adjourned at 17:30 hours.
