STAFF SELECTION COMMISSION - PUBLIC ADVISORY
Notice Ref: SSC-NR-2026-ADV-04
Date of Release: 10 February 2026

Subject: Combined Graduate Level Examination 2026 Tier-| Schedule

1. Eligible candidates holding a recognized University Degree with minimum 55% marks may apply.
2. Candidates must be between 18 and 30 years of age on 01 January 2026.

3. Application Fee: Rs. 100 (Exempted for Women and SC/ST candidates).

4. Closing Date for Online Applications: 25 April 2026.

5. Required Documents: Graduation Certificate, Passport Photo, Scanned Signature.

6. Website: https://ssc.gov.in/portal
